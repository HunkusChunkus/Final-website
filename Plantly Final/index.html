Welcome to plantly

Here at plantly we aim to teach people about the native plants of New Zealand and how to take care of plants. Whether you have a big backyard, a small balcony, or just want to learn more about the trees and flowers around you, there is something here for everyone.

Why Look After Native Plants?

New Zealand's native plants have grown here for millions of years, and many are found nowhere else in the world. They give food and shelter to native birds, insects and lizards, so looking after them helps the whole environment.

Five Easy Care Tips

  1. Pick plants that suit your spot: sun or shade, wet or dry, coast or inland.
  2. Plant in autumn or winter so the roots settle in before summer. In frosty areas, wait until spring.
  3. Water new plants deeply during dry spells in their first summer.
  4. Spread mulch around the plant to keep the soil moist and stop weeds.
  5. Go easy on fertiliser. Most natives need very little.
