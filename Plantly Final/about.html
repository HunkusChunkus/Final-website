About Plantly

Plantly is a website that teaches people about the native plants of New Zealand and how to take care of them.

Why Native Plants?

Native plants evolved in New Zealand, so they suit our weather and soils. They also feed and shelter native birds, insects and lizards. When we look after native plants, we look after the wildlife that depends on them.

Many native plants are also slow to come back once they are gone. Some, like kauri, can take hundreds of years to grow to full size, so protecting the ones we already have is just as important as planting new ones.

How You Can Help

  • Plant natives in your own garden, even a small pot makes a difference.
  • Clean your shoes before and after bush walks to help stop the spread of plant diseases.
  • Report sightings of myrtle rust or other plant diseases to the Ministry for Primary Industries.
  • Join a local planting day, like our Torbay initiative on the blog page.

Words You Might See

  • Native: A plant that grows naturally in New Zealand without being brought here by people.
  • Endemic: A plant that is found only in New Zealand and nowhere else in the world.
  • Mulch: A layer of bark, leaves or compost on top of the soil. It keeps the soil moist and stops weeds.
  • Rongoa: Traditional Maori medicine, which often uses native plants.
  • Kaitiakitanga: Guardianship. Looking after the environment so it stays healthy for the future.
