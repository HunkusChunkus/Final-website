What Plantly Offers

Everything on Plantly is free to read. Here is what you can find.

  • Plant Profiles: Meet a native plants and see what it needs to grow well. Find it on the Portfolio page.
  • Care Guides: Simple tips on planting, watering and mulching, and what to do in each season. Find it on the Home page.
  • Photo Gallery: See what native plants look like so you can spot them in the wild. Find it on the Gallery page.
  • Plant Blog: Short posts about planting, protecting forests and helping native birds. Find it on the Blog page.
  • Ask A Question: Send us your plant question using the contact form. Find it on the Contact page.
